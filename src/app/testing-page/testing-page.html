Big Buck Bunny — EzVidAng Feature Showcase

128,403 views • Published Jul 2, 2026

This player is wired up with every control shipped by ez-vid-ang: adaptive HLS streaming, chapter markers, multi-language subtitles, a YouTube-style settings panel, keyboard shortcuts, screenshot capture, download, Picture-in-Picture, remote playback (Cast/AirPlay), and cinema mode.

Right-click the player for a custom context menu. Open the settings gear for playback speed, loop, and Picture-in-Picture toggles. Press the cinema mode button in the controls bar to dim the page and expand the player edge-to-edge, the same way YouTube's theater mode works.

About this demo

  • Adaptive streaming HLS via hls.js with automatic quality switching through eva-quality-selector.
  • Chapters Scrub-bar markers, an active-chapter indicator, and a toggleable chapter list panel.
  • Subtitles & tracks Multi-language subtitle tracks switchable from eva-track-selector.
  • Persistence Volume, playback speed, cinema mode, and loop are restored from local storage on reload.
  • Keyboard shortcuts J/L and arrow-key seeking, with an on-screen cheat sheet overlay.
  • Cinema mode Dims the page and floats the player above the backdrop, theater-mode style.
